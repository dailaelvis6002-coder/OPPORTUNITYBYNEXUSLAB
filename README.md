## Abstract & Overview

**Opportunity by Nexus Lab** is an educational opportunities platform designed to help talented students discover scholarships and other opportunities to pursue their academic and professional ambitions, regardless of their financial circumstances. The project focuses particularly on high-achieving students from low-income backgrounds in Burkina Faso and across Africa.

Access to educational opportunities is not determined by academic ability alone. Students also need access to reliable information about scholarships, eligibility requirements, application deadlines, required documents and financial support. Yet this information is often scattered across university websites, institutional announcements and other online sources. For students who lack access to educational guidance or professional networks, finding and evaluating suitable opportunities can be difficult.

As a result, talented students may miss opportunities that could help them continue their education.

Opportunity by Nexus Lab aims to address this information gap by bringing educational opportunities together in one organised and accessible platform.

Rather than creating another source of scattered information, the project seeks to simplify the discovery process and help students identify opportunities that match their academic goals and circumstances.

## The Problem

Many students have the ability and motivation to pursue higher education but face financial barriers and limited access to information about available funding.

Finding a suitable scholarship often requires students to answer several questions:

- Which universities and organisations offer relevant opportunities?
- Who is eligible to apply?
- What expenses does the scholarship cover?
- Which documents are required?
- When does the application period close?
- Where can the official application information be found?

When these details are difficult to locate or compare, students may spend considerable time searching for information without knowing which opportunities are worth pursuing.

Opportunity by Nexus Lab addresses this practical challenge by making educational opportunity information easier to discover, navigate and evaluate.

## Mission

The mission of Opportunity by Nexus Lab is to make educational opportunities more accessible by reducing the information barriers that prevent talented, financially disadvantaged students from discovering pathways to further education.

The project is guided by the belief that a student's financial circumstances should not determine whether they can discover opportunities to fulfil their academic potential.

## Target Audience

The platform is designed primarily for:

- High-achieving students from low-income backgrounds.
- Students in Burkina Faso and across Africa seeking educational funding.
- Students looking for undergraduate scholarships and other learning opportunities.
- Girls and young women seeking relevant educational programmes.
- Students who need a clearer and more organised way to discover opportunities.

Although the project prioritises students facing financial barriers, the platform's broader purpose is to make relevant educational information easier to access.

## Key Features

1. **Educational Opportunities Catalogue**
The platform is designed to organise relevant educational opportunities in one place, with scholarships as its primary focus. Other categories may include training programmes, internships and competitions where relevant opportunities are available.

2. **Search and Filtering**
Search and filtering are intended to help users navigate the catalogue and identify opportunities relevant to their interests and educational circumstances.

3. **Detailed Opportunity Listings**
Opportunity listings are intended to provide essential information in a consistent format, including:
- University or organisation offering the opportunity.
- Scholarship or programme name.
- Eligible study level.
- Eligibility requirements.
- Expenses covered.
- Application deadline.
- Required documents.

This structure helps students assess an opportunity before investing time in an application.

4. **Opportunities for Girls**
A dedicated section is intended to highlight opportunities specifically designed for girls and young women, making relevant programmes easier to identify.

5. **Administrative Management**
The platform is designed around an administrative model in which authorised administrators manage opportunity listings. Visitors can explore the catalogue without needing to create an account.

6. **Accessible User Experience**
The website prioritises clear navigation, readable information and a consistent visual identity. Its purpose is to help visitors find relevant information without unnecessary complexity.

## Design Principles

Opportunity by Nexus Lab is guided by three principles.

- **Accessibility:** Educational opportunity information should be easier to discover, particularly for students who lack extensive guidance or professional connections.
- **Clarity:** Essential information should be presented in a structured and understandable format.
- **Reliability:** Information about eligibility, funding and deadlines should be based on verifiable sources rather than unsupported claims.

The design supports the project's mission by prioritising usability and the quality of information over unnecessary visual complexity.

## Information Reliability

The usefulness of an educational opportunities platform depends on the accuracy and relevance of its listings.

Whenever possible, opportunity information should be checked against official university websites, scholarship providers and programme announcements. Eligibility conditions, application deadlines and funding details should not be invented or presented as confirmed without reliable evidence.

Students should consult the official provider's website before applying, particularly when requirements or deadlines may change.

Opportunity by Nexus Lab helps users discover opportunities; it does not guarantee admission, scholarship awards or financial assistance.

Opportunity by Nexus Lab was developed through a vibe-coding approach, combining my own ideas, decisions and knowledge with the coding assistance and capabilities of Google AI Studio.

I defined the project's purpose and intended user experience while using AI-assisted development to help translate the concept into a website.

The development approach reflects my interest in using technology to address practical educational challenges. AI-assisted development was a tool in the process, not a substitute for the project's underlying purpose or the need to evaluate the resulting application.

## Social Impact

Opportunity by Nexus Lab seeks to reduce the distance between educational opportunities and the students who could benefit from them.

By organising relevant information and making it easier to navigate, the platform aims to help students discover programmes they might otherwise overlook, understand their requirements and make more informed decisions about their educational futures.

Its potential contribution lies in improving access to information, not promising outcomes that the platform cannot control.

The project connects software development with a broader educational concern: talented students should have a fairer chance of discovering pathways to further education, even when their financial circumstances limit their options.

## Future Development

The project can evolve through improvements to the catalogue, search experience, information management and verification processes.

Future enhancements will depend on identified user needs, technical feasibility and the project's development priorities.

The long-term objective is to maintain a useful, accessible and trustworthy resource for students seeking educational opportunities.
